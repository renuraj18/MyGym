MyGym privacy policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

MyGym is an Android app that helps gym owners keep track of members and fee payments. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information stored by the app

The gym owner enters gym details, member names, WhatsApp numbers, gender, joining dates, plans, payments and optional member photos. This information is stored only in the app's database on the owner's phone. It is never sent to the developer or to the license server.

Google account and Google Drive

If the owner chooses to turn on backup, the app asks them to sign in with Google. The app requests only the drive.file permission, which lets it create and use the "MyGym Backup" spreadsheet in the owner's own Google Drive. It cannot see or change any other files. The app uses the Google Sheets and Google Drive APIs only to write the backup and to read it back when the owner chooses Restore. The owner's email address is shown in the app so they know which account is connected. Google account information is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

MyGym's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

License check

To run the 7-day free trial and check license keys, the app sends the MyGym license server this phone's Android device ID, phone model and app version, and any license key entered. Nothing else is sent: no gym, member or payment information. This lets one license key work on one phone and stops the trial from restarting after a reinstall.

WhatsApp

Reminders and receipts are sent by opening WhatsApp with the message already typed. The owner sends each message themselves. The app does not use a WhatsApp API and does not send messages on its own.

Camera, photos and fingerprint

The camera and photo gallery are used only when the owner adds a member photo. Photos stay on the phone and are not uploaded, including to the Google backup. Fingerprint unlock uses Android's built-in biometric system; the app never receives fingerprint data.

Sharing

MyGym does not sell, rent or share any information. There are no ads and no analytics.

Deleting data

The owner can delete all data by uninstalling the app. The backup spreadsheet can be deleted from Google Drive, and the app's access can be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
