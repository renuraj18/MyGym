MyGym

An Android app for gym owners to manage members and fee payments across one or more gyms.

  • Track members, plans (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, yearly or custom) and due dates
  • Record GPay/UPI and cash payments with receipt numbers and part-payments
  • Send payment reminders and receipts through WhatsApp
  • Monthly reports by gym, with PDF and Google Sheets export
  • Optional backup to a Google Sheet in the owner's own Google Drive
  • App lock with PIN and fingerprint

All gym data is stored on the phone. See the privacy policy and terms of use.
